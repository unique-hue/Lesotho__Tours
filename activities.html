Activities

Ride a Basotho pony through villages and over mountain paths. Trips can be a few hours or several days.

Drive the steep, winding gravel road up the Drakensberg escarpment. The views are amazing.

Walk to waterfalls, valleys and hilltop views. Take water, warm clothes and a guide for long routes.

Lesotho has snow in winter, and you can ski or snowboard at a mountain resort.

Visit Thaba Bosiu, meet local people and see the famous Basotho blankets and straw hats.

Pony trekking in the Maloti Mountains
