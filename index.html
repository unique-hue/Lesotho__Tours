Why visit Lesotho?

Lesotho is a small mountain country surrounded by South Africa. It is known as the Kingdom in the Sky because the whole country sits high up in the mountains.

You can ride a pony through quiet villages, drive the winding Sani Pass, hike to big waterfalls and learn about Basotho culture.

Quick facts

  • Capital Maseru
  • Money Loti (M)
  • Languages Sesotho and English
  • Nickname Kingdom in the Sky
